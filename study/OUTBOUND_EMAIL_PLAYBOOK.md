# Cards Club — 7 Outbound Campaign Angles

Use these as segment-specific templates, not as one generic sequence for all prospects. Personalize company context, keep first CTA low-friction, and measure positive reply / RFQ / sample rates rather than open rate alone.

---

## 1) Distributor Acquisition
**Subject:** Playing cards for {{Country}}  
**Preview:** Standard, cultural and private-label decks manufactured in Egypt.

Hi {{FirstName}},

Cards Club manufactures premium playing cards in Egypt for retail, distribution and private-label programs.

We offer standard Bridge decks, cultural collections and fully customized editions. We're opening selected distribution partnerships in {{Country}}, and {{Company}} looks relevant to the category.

Would it be useful if I sent our distributor range, MOQ and export pricing?

**CTA:** Reply `SEND RANGE`.

## Sequence rules
- Run micro-campaigns by **country × segment × offer**.
- Start with **1–2 decision makers per account**.
- Default pilot sequence: **3 messages total** before adding more touchpoints.
- Keep sending volume controlled per mailbox.
- Track: delivered, bounce, reply, positive reply, RFQ, sample, PO, reorder.
- Do not lead with a calendar link if a simpler CTA can get a response.
- Never promise customs advantages until product-specific origin/tariff treatment is verified.
